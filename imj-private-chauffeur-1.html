About us

A name clients trust

For over six years, IMJ Private Chauffeur has driven clients across Nigeria and the diaspora. Many are returning home or visiting from abroad, and they need a driver they can rely on from the moment they land.

We built our name on one promise: a safe, comfortable and dependable journey, delivered by professional people.

  • Reliable. We plan around your schedule so you are never left waiting.
